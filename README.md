# Data Professional Survey Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard that breaks down survey responses from data professionals, covering salary, location, job satisfaction, the difficulty of entering the field, and favourite programming languages.

## Dataset
Data professional survey dataset 630 responses.

## Tools
Power BI Desktop, Power Query

## What I Did
- Cleaned the dataset in Power Query: removed unused columns and grouped inconsistent entries into standard categories across 4-5 columns
- Converted reported salary ranges into mean values so salary could be analysed by industry and job title
- Built a one-page interactive dashboard with:
  - KPI cards: number of survey takers and average age
  - Bar charts: average salary by industry and by job title
  - Treemap: country of survey takers
  - Gauges: happiness with work/life balance and with salary
  - Doughnut chart: difficulty of breaking into the field
  - Column chart: favourite programming language by job title

## Questions Explored
- Which industries and job titles have the highest average salary?
- Where are the survey takers located?
- How satisfied are data professionals with their salary and work/life balance?
- How difficult do people find it to break into the field?
- Which programming languages do data professionals prefer?
